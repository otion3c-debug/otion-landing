Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

1. What We Collect

ScheduleLink-Go (operated by Otion LLC) collects only what we need to make scheduling work: your name and email when you sign up, phone number and message content for SMS features, calendar tokens so we can read availability and write events, and the bookings made through your link.

Payment information is processed securely through Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide and improve the Service: schedule appointments, send SMS confirmations and reminders, enable two-way messaging between businesses and customers, process payments, sync with Google and Microsoft calendars, and provide customer support.

3. SMS & Text Messaging

ScheduleLink-Go uses SMS to send appointment confirmations, reminders, and facilitate communication between service providers and their clients.

  • Message frequency varies based on activity. You typically receive 2–6 messages per booking (confirmation, reminders, and two-way communication).
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP, CANCEL, END, or UNSUBSCRIBE at any time to stop receiving SMS. You will receive one final confirmation message.
  • Help: Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply. Contact your carrier for pricing.
  • Marketing: We do not send marketing or promotional SMS without your separate consent.

Mobile information and messaging consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the Service: Twilio (SMS delivery), Stripe (payments), Google and Microsoft (calendar APIs), Vercel (hosting), Zoho (email notifications).
